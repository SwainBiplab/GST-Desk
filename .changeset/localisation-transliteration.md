Six languages and typing names in your own script. The language of the screens switches at once from the toolbar with no reload and is remembered on the device. Hindi, Marathi, Tamil, Bengali and Gujarati cover the navigation, the toolbar, the inbox and error messages so far (the rest of each screen shows in English until translated); printed documents and payment reminders are complete in all six. Party and item names can be typed in Roman letters and turned into Devanagari, Tamil, Bengali or Gujarati as you type, with the likeliest spellings offered; the name is kept in your script and also by its Roman spelling, so search finds it either way. A party can have its own language, used for their copy of a document and for reminders sent to them. Dates, lakh grouping, the rupee sign and financial years follow Indian conventions in every language. For the team: `pnpm i18n:check`, `i18n:draft` and `i18n:extract` keep the catalogues complete, translation keys are checked by the compiler, and text typed straight into a screen is a lint error.
