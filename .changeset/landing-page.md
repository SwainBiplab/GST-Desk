A landing page for the web app at `/landing/`. A first-time web visitor who opens the address is sent there: it shows what GST Desk does, how it compares with Tally, Vyapar, Zoho Books and myBillBook (taken from each vendor's own pages, with "not stated" where they say nothing), what works in the beta, and what is planned. The desktop app, an installed web app, and anyone who has used the app before go straight to the app. The page is static files with no third-party requests and has its own accessibility and first-visit tests.
