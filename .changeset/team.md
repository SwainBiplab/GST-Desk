Roles and permissions (prompt 42). One permission matrix over modules and actions, shared by the app, the API and the sync write path; built-in roles (owner, admin, accountant, counter, store, view only) and an organisation's own roles. Team settings: invite by e-mail or phone (with an accountant preset), change roles, remove members, stop a member's device, see sign-ins. With approvals on, cancellations and revisions of issued documents by people who cannot approve wait in the owners' inbox; approving applies them on the server as the person who asked. The command bar, the nav and the main buttons follow the role; the audit trail shows people by name with a per-person summary and their sign-ins.
