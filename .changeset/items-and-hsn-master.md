Items, units and the HSN/SAC master. Item catalogue with barcode check digits, UQC and custom units, HSN suggestions, bulk edit with preview and undo, a rate change assistant, a signed and versioned HSN master (bundled, with an optional update address), and a CSV import wizard with column mapping, dry run, progress and cancel for items and parties. The import reads Indian number styles and CSV exports neutralise formulas.
