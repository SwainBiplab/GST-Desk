Payments, receipts, allocations and party credit. Receive and make payments with automatic oldest-first or manual allocation, cheque details, round-off write-off up to 99 paise, TCS on receipt, and the remainder kept as party credit that can be applied to the next invoice. Receipt vouchers with their own series (print, share, revise on edit), undo within 30 seconds, edit and delete with audit, a payments list with filters and CSV export, a day book in Shop, bank and UPI accounts with validation, and unpaid, partly paid, paid, overpaid and overdue status on every document. The demo data now posts to the ledger, and a test reconciles party balance, document balances and ledger for every seeded party.
