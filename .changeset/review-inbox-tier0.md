The review inbox. The app now notices things for you and puts them in one place, and nothing changes until you accept it: parties and items that look like duplicates (accepting merges them), items with no HSN (accepting fills in the HSN and GST rate), GST rate changes in the master, customers with overdue invoices (with a reminder drafted in their language), items running low, drafts nobody has touched for a week, and failed backups. Accept, change then accept, reject with a reason, or snooze; "Accept many" shows a preview first; J, K, A, E, R, S and O work from the keyboard, so a queue clears with repeated presses of A. Shop shows the same items as simple cards. The checks run when the app opens, every six hours, and a moment after you save a party or item; each finding is raised once and a finished one is never raised again. One optional automatic rule (accept very sure HSN suggestions) is off until you turn it on. A product's duplicate can now be merged into another, moving its stock and history.
