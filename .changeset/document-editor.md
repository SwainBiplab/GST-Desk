The document editor. A spreadsheet-style grid on Glide Data Grid with a live preview and trace panel for all document types: party and item pickers with quick-create, paste, reorder, per-line notes, invoice discount, round-off, TCS and TDS, sanitised rich-text notes and terms, attachments, 2 s draft autosave and recovery, duplicate and convert, purchase bills with input-credit and reverse-charge self-invoices, and per-year document numbering that never reuses a number.
