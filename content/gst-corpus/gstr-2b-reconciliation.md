# GSTR-2B

GSTR-2B is an auto-drafted, static statement of input tax credit generated for a recipient on the fourteenth day of each month from the GSTR-1, IFF, GSTR-5 and GSTR-6 filed by suppliers between the previous month's filing cut-off and this one. It lists credit as available or not available, with reasons (the supplier's return filed late, the recipient's state differing from the place of supply, the invoice dated more than a year before, or credit blocked under section 16(4)), and it does not change once generated. Since 1 January 2022 credit may be taken only on invoices appearing in it (section 16(2)(aa), rule 36(4)).

# GSTR-2A

GSTR-2A is a dynamic view of the same supplier filings, updated as suppliers file or amend. It is for reference: a supplier's later filing shows there, and the invoice then appears in the next month's GSTR-2B.

# Common reasons the books and GSTR-2B disagree

The supplier has not filed GSTR-1, or filed it after the cut-off, so the invoice will appear in a later month (credit waits). The supplier reported the invoice in a different period (credit is taken in the month it appears). The invoice number or date differ by a typo on one side (match by value and GSTIN, then fix the book entry or ask the supplier to amend). The amount or tax differs (the supplier may have amended; take credit at the reported amount and resolve the difference with the supplier). The supplier used the wrong GSTIN of the recipient, so the invoice appears in another registration's statement. The supply is marked as reverse charge, so the credit is through table 4(A)(3), not from the supplier's filing. The place of supply differs from the recipient's state and the credit is marked unavailable. The invoice was cancelled by the supplier through a credit note, which appears separately.

# What to do with each class

Matched entries are taken as credit. Entries only in GSTR-2B with no book bill are checked against the supplier (a missing purchase in the books, or a wrong GSTIN at the supplier's end). Book bills not in GSTR-2B are deferred: the credit is not taken until the entry appears, and the supplier is reminded to file. Differences in value are taken at the lower of the two until resolved. Entries marked as credit not available are reversed in table 4(B).
