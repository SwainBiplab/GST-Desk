# Why it matters

A supply within a state attracts central and state (or union territory) tax; a supply from one state to another attracts integrated tax. The place of supply, with the location of the supplier, decides which.

# Goods (section 10)

Where the supply involves movement of goods, the place of supply is the location of the goods when the movement ends for delivery to the recipient. Where goods are delivered to a third person on the direction of the buyer (a bill-to-ship-to supply), the buyer's principal place of business is the place of supply. Where there is no movement, it is the location of the goods at delivery. Goods assembled or installed at a site are supplied at that site; goods supplied on board a conveyance are supplied where the goods were taken on board. For a supply to an unregistered person where the address on the invoice is recorded, the address on the invoice is the place of supply (from 1 October 2023).

# Services, the general rule (section 12(2))

A service supplied to a registered person is supplied at the recipient's location; a service to an unregistered person is supplied at the recipient's address on record, or else at the supplier's location.

# Services with their own rules (section 12(3) to (14))

Services relating to immovable property (architects, estate agents, hotels, construction) are supplied where the property is; restaurant, personal grooming, fitness and health services where they are performed; training and performance appraisal to an unregistered person where performed; admission to events where the event is held; organisation of events to an unregistered person where held; transportation of goods to an unregistered person where the goods are handed over (and, from 1 October 2023, the recipient's location for a registered person); passenger transport where the passenger embarks; services on board a conveyance at the first scheduled departure; telecommunication by the address of the fixed line or the billing address; banking and financial services at the recipient's address on record; insurance at the recipient's location when registered, else the address on record; and advertisement services to the government by the state covered.
