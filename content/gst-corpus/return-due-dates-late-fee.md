# GSTR-1 (statement of outward supplies, section 37)

A monthly filer furnishes GSTR-1 for a month by the eleventh day of the following month. A person under the quarterly return monthly payment (QRMP) scheme furnishes GSTR-1 for a quarter by the thirteenth day of the month following the quarter, and may report B2B invoices, credit notes and debit notes for the first and second months of the quarter through the invoice furnishing facility (IFF) by the thirteenth of the following month, up to fifty lakh rupees of value per month. GSTR-1 cannot be furnished if the GSTR-1 of an earlier period is pending. Since 1 August 2024 an amendment statement, GSTR-1A, allows additions or amendments for the same period before GSTR-3B is filed.

# GSTR-3B (return, section 39)

A monthly filer furnishes GSTR-3B and pays the tax by the twentieth day of the following month. A QRMP filer furnishes GSTR-3B for the quarter by the twenty-second day of the month following the quarter for the states and union territories of the southern and western group (Chhattisgarh, Madhya Pradesh, Gujarat, Maharashtra, Karnataka, Goa, Kerala, Tamil Nadu, Telangana, Andhra Pradesh, Daman and Diu, Dadra and Nagar Haveli, Puducherry, Andaman and Nicobar Islands, Lakshadweep) and by the twenty-fourth day for the rest, and pays tax for the first two months of the quarter by the twenty-fifth of the following month in form PMT-06. GSTR-3B cannot be furnished if the GSTR-3B of an earlier period, or the GSTR-1 for the same period, is pending. A return filed after three years from its due date is barred (sections 37(5), 39(11), inserted by the Finance Act, 2023, effective 1 October 2023).

# Annual return (section 44)

Every registered person other than an input service distributor, a person deducting or collecting tax at source, a casual taxable person and a non-resident taxable person furnishes the annual return GSTR-9 for a financial year by the thirty-first day of December following that year. The requirement is waived for a financial year for persons with aggregate turnover up to two crore rupees (by notification, year by year). The reconciliation statement GSTR-9C is furnished with it by persons with aggregate turnover above five crore rupees.

# Late fee (section 47, notifications 19/2021, 20/2021 central tax)

The late fee under the Act is one hundred rupees per day under the central law and the same under the state law, capped at five thousand rupees each. The notifications reduce it: for GSTR-3B and GSTR-1 from the June 2021 period, twenty-five rupees per day under each law (ten rupees per day for a nil return), capped per return at two hundred and fifty rupees each (nil), one thousand rupees each (aggregate turnover up to one and a half crore rupees), two thousand five hundred rupees each (turnover above one and a half and up to five crore rupees), and five thousand rupees each above that. The annual return's late fee is one hundred rupees per day under each law, capped at a quarter per cent of turnover in the state or union territory, with lower rates notified for turnover up to twenty crore rupees from 2022-23.

# Interest on late payment (section 50)

Interest at eighteen per cent per annum is payable on tax paid after the due date, computed on the net tax liability paid through the electronic cash ledger (for returns filed late other than after proceedings under sections 73 or 74). Interest at twenty-four per cent applies to credit wrongly availed and utilised.
