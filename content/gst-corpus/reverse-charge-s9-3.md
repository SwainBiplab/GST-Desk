# Services on which the recipient pays (section 9(3), notification 13/2017 central tax (rate))

The recipient pays the tax, rather than the supplier, on services of a goods transport agency to a registered business (unless the agency opts to pay at twelve per cent), legal services by an advocate or firm of advocates to a business, services of an arbitral tribunal, sponsorship services to a body corporate or partnership, specified services of the central or state government or a local authority to a business (other than renting of immovable property to a registered person, which is also reverse charge, and a few others), services of a director to the company, services of an insurance agent, recovery agent, music composer or author of copyright, security services by a person other than a body corporate to a registered person, renting of a motor vehicle designed to carry passengers by a non-body corporate charging five per cent to a body corporate, lending of securities, renting of commercial property by an unregistered person to a registered person (from 10 October 2024), and import of services from outside India (section 5(3) of the IGST Act).

# Goods on which the recipient pays (notification 4/2017 central tax (rate))

Cashew nuts not shelled, bidi wrapper leaves, tobacco leaves, silk yarn, raw cotton, and supply of lottery by the state, among others, when supplied by an agriculturist or other specified supplier to a registered person; used vehicles, seized goods and scrap sold by the government to a registered person; and metal scrap from an unregistered supplier to a registered person (from 10 October 2024).

# Purchases from unregistered persons (section 9(4))

The general reverse charge on every purchase from an unregistered supplier was withdrawn; from 1 April 2019 it applies only to notified recipients, namely promoters of real estate projects on cement, capital goods and the shortfall of inputs below eighty per cent from registered suppliers.

# Self-invoice and credit

The recipient paying tax under reverse charge issues an invoice themselves (section 31(3)(f)) within thirty days of receipt of the supply (rule 47A, from 1 November 2024) and a payment voucher when paying the supplier. The tax is paid in cash (not through credit) and, once paid, can be taken as input tax credit subject to the usual conditions, in the same month. The reverse charge liability is reported in table 3.1(d) of GSTR-3B and the credit in table 4(A)(3).
