# When a tax invoice is issued (section 31)

A registered person supplying taxable goods issues the tax invoice before or at the time of removal of the goods (where the supply involves movement) or their delivery or making available to the recipient. For services the invoice is issued within thirty days of the supply of the service (forty-five days for banks, insurers and financial institutions). A supplier of exempt goods or services, and a person paying tax under the composition scheme, issues a bill of supply instead of a tax invoice.

# What a tax invoice must contain (rule 46)

The name, address and GSTIN of the supplier; a consecutive serial number of up to sixteen characters, unique for a financial year, containing only letters, numerals and the characters hyphen, dash and slash; the date of issue; the name, address and GSTIN or UIN of the recipient when registered; for an unregistered recipient and a value of fifty thousand rupees or more, the recipient's name, address, delivery address and state with its code; the HSN code of the goods or the SAC of the services; the description, quantity and unit of the goods; the total value; the taxable value after discount or abatement; the rate of tax (central, state or union territory, integrated, cess); the amount of tax charged; the place of supply with the name of the state for an inter-state supply; the delivery address when it differs from the place of supply; whether tax is payable on reverse charge; and the signature or digital signature of the supplier or an authorised representative. An e-invoice carries the quick response code with the invoice reference number instead of the signature.

# HSN digits on invoices (notification 78/2020 central tax)

From 1 April 2021 a registered person with aggregate turnover up to five crore rupees in the preceding financial year shows four digits of HSN on invoices to registered persons (optional for supplies to unregistered persons), and one with turnover above five crore rupees shows six digits on every invoice. Eight digits apply to the goods notified for export and chemicals.

# Invoice-cum-bill of supply (rule 46A)

Where a registered person supplies taxable and exempt goods or services to an unregistered person in one transaction, a single invoice-cum-bill of supply may be issued for all the supplies.

# Bill of supply (rule 49)

A bill of supply carries the supplier's name, address and GSTIN, a consecutive serial number, the date, the recipient's details when registered, the HSN or SAC, the description, the value after discount and the signature. A composition supplier prints the words "composition taxable person, not eligible to collect tax on supplies" on it.

# Small invoices (rule 46, fourth proviso)

A registered person need not issue a tax invoice for a supply valued at less than two hundred rupees to an unregistered recipient who does not ask for one, and may issue one consolidated invoice for such supplies at the end of each day.
