# Who may opt in

A registered person whose aggregate turnover in the preceding financial year did not exceed one and a half crore rupees (seventy-five lakh rupees in Arunachal Pradesh, Manipur, Meghalaya, Mizoram, Nagaland, Sikkim, Tripura and Uttarakhand) may pay tax under the composition scheme instead of the regular scheme. The person must not supply goods or services not taxable under the Act, make inter-state outward supplies, supply through an e-commerce operator who collects tax at source (relaxed for goods from 1 October 2023), be a manufacturer of notified goods (ice cream, pan masala, tobacco, aerated water, fly ash bricks and building bricks), or be a casual or non-resident taxable person. A supplier of goods may also supply services up to ten per cent of turnover or five lakh rupees, whichever is higher. A separate scheme under section 10(2A) covers service providers with turnover up to fifty lakh rupees.

# Rates

Manufacturers pay one per cent of turnover in the state (half under the central law and half under the state law); restaurants not serving alcohol pay five per cent; other suppliers (traders) pay one per cent of turnover of taxable supplies of goods and services; service providers under section 10(2A) pay six per cent. A composition taxpayer cannot collect tax from customers, cannot take input tax credit, issues a bill of supply rather than a tax invoice, and must mention the composition status on the bill and at the place of business.

# Returns and payment

A composition taxpayer pays tax quarterly through form CMP-08 by the eighteenth day of the month following the quarter, and furnishes the annual return GSTR-4 for the financial year by the thirtieth day of June following that year (thirtieth of April for years up to 2023-24). Reverse charge tax is paid at the regular rates in addition.

# Opting in and out

The option is taken for a financial year in form CMP-02 before the year begins (or at registration), and lapses from the day the turnover crosses the limit, when the person files CMP-04 within seven days. A person moving to the regular scheme may take credit on stock held, by a statement in ITC-01 within thirty days.
