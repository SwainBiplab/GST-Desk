# Conditions for taking input tax credit (section 16(2))

A registered person may take credit of input tax on a supply only if all of these hold: the person has a tax invoice, debit note or other prescribed document; the supplier has furnished the details of that invoice or debit note in their statement of outward supplies (GSTR-1 or the invoice furnishing facility) and the details have been communicated to the recipient in GSTR-2B; the goods or services have been received (goods delivered to a third person on the recipient's direction count as received, and goods received in lots are credited on the last lot); the credit is not restricted in GSTR-2B under section 38; the tax charged has actually been paid to the government; and the recipient has furnished the return under section 39. Input credit is therefore decided against GSTR-2B: an invoice the supplier has not reported, or has reported in a later period, is not available until it appears there.

# Payment to the supplier within 180 days

Where the recipient fails to pay the supplier the value of the supply along with the tax within one hundred and eighty days of the date of the invoice, an amount equal to the credit taken is added to the recipient's output tax liability with interest (rule 37). The credit may be taken again once the payment is made. This does not apply to supplies taxed on reverse charge or where the value is not paid because of a scheme of the tax law.

# Time limit for taking credit (section 16(4))

Credit on an invoice or debit note for a financial year cannot be taken after the thirtieth day of November following the end of that financial year or the furnishing of the relevant annual return, whichever is earlier. For invoices of the years 2017-18 to 2020-21 a one-time relaxation allowed credit in any return filed up to 30 November 2021 (section 16(5), inserted by the Finance (No. 2) Act, 2024).

# Blocked credits (section 17(5))

No credit is available on motor vehicles for carrying persons with seating capacity up to thirteen (including the driver) unless used for further supply, passenger transport or driving training; on vessels and aircraft with similar exceptions; on food and beverages, outdoor catering, beauty treatment, health services, cosmetic and plastic surgery, club memberships, health and fitness centres, travel benefits to employees on vacation, and life and health insurance, unless the supply is used for making an outward supply of the same category or is obligatory for an employer under a law; on works contract services for construction of immovable property (other than plant and machinery) except when it is an input for further works contract; on goods or services received for construction of immovable property on own account; on goods or services on which composition tax was paid; on goods lost, stolen, destroyed, written off or given as gifts or free samples; on goods or services used for personal consumption; and on any tax paid as penalty or on account of fraud.

# Documents for credit (rule 36)

Credit is taken on a tax invoice, a debit note, a bill of entry, an ISD invoice, or a self-invoice for a reverse-charge supply, carrying the prescribed particulars. Credit on invoices not furnished by the supplier in GSTR-1 and not communicated in GSTR-2B is not available (rule 36(4), as substituted from 1 January 2022).
