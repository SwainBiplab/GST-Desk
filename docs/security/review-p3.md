# Security review: Phase 3

Scope: the code shipped by prompts 40 to 48 (the cloud API with better-auth and Postgres row-level security,
sync through PowerSync, roles and permissions, the phone app, WhatsApp and e-mail dispatch with recurring invoices,
payment links and UPI QR through Razorpay, GST filing through the GSP and the CA board, sharing with an
accountant and the customer's pages, and the Tier 2 cloud AI gateway), reviewed against the checklist in
`prompts/cross-cutting/90-security-review.md` on 2026-11-07. The threat model was kept current prompt by prompt
(`docs/security/threat-model.md`: the API, object storage, sync, team and permissions, shares, customer links, the
AI gateway, export packs and the phone app each have a row) and its "out of scope" line now says so.

## Result

No open Must findings. Three Should findings were fixed in this review (findings 1 to 3); the rest are in the
backlog with ids. Semgrep and gitleaks still could not be run here (B-089): a regex scan over every commit found
nothing but two base64 images that match a token's shape. `cargo audit` runs on both crates and `pnpm audit --prod`
reports two moderate advisories in build-time paths.

## Residual risk

- The customer's page and the accountant's client view are read through the cloud API; their security rests on the
  API's row-level security and the share's narrowing, both tested, and on `SECRETS_KEY` for the sealed tokens.
- A person's own cloud AI key lives in the desktop webview while a call runs (finding 4).
- Nothing in Phase 3 has met a real provider: WhatsApp, Resend, Razorpay, a GSP, PowerSync in production, Firebase
  and Anthropic were all driven through mocks and fixtures; each has a "not run" record under `docs/qa` or
  `docs/compliance`, and the first real run is where the remaining surprises are.
- Data at rest on the device is still not encrypted (B-087).
