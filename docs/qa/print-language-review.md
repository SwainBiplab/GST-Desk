# Print wording: review by native speakers

The printed words for Hindi, Tamil, Bengali, Gujarati and Marathi (`packages/i18n/src/print.ts`) and the share messages are first drafts. A printed invoice goes to a customer, so each language needs a review by someone who reads it daily, ideally an accountant or a shop owner.

How: Settings → Print, pick the language, open the designer preview for each document type (tax invoice, bill of supply, credit note, export, reverse charge), and read every word on the page.

| Gujarati |          |      |                |

Things to check in particular: the three Rule 48 copy labels, "Reverse Charge", the export wording, "Authorised Signatory", "Taxable", the tax heads (CGST, SGST, IGST, UTGST, Cess), and the amount-in-words line (only Hindi and English words exist today; Marathi prints the Hindi words, Tamil, Bengali and Gujarati the English words).

A business can override any word in any language from the designer without waiting for a release.
