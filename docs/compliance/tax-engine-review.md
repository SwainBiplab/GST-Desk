## Open points for the reviewer

1. **B2CL threshold.** The engine uses Rs 1 lakh for invoices dated on or after 1 Aug 2024 and Rs 2.5 lakh before. Confirm against the notification in force.
2. **206C(1H) sunset.** Implemented as "no TCS on or after 1 Oct 2024". Confirm the treatment of advances received earlier for supplies made later.
3. **Tax-inclusive drift.** Totals may differ from the entered inclusive price by up to two paise (CGST and SGST are kept equal). Confirm this is acceptable on printed invoices.
4. **Fractional head rates.** 0.25% splits into 0.125% heads; amounts are correct but the rate cannot be stored in an integer basis-point column.
5. **Deemed exports** are taxed with IGST and treated as B2B. Confirm.
6. **Composition purchases** and **RCM on sales** are not modelled beyond the flags above.
