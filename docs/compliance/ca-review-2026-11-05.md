# Compliance review of GST Desk through prompt 32 (5 November 2026)

**Who did this.** This review was carried out by Claude (an AI assistant) at the business owner's request, working in the role of a chartered accountant reviewing the tax logic. Claude is not a member of ICAI and this document is **not a statutory sign-off**. It is a rigorous technical review of the rules the software implements against the CGST Act and Rules, the IGST Act, the Income-tax Act and the GSTN portal behaviour as known to the reviewer, with the section references given so a licensed accountant can confirm or overturn each point quickly. Every signature line in `docs/compliance/` stays blank.

**What was reviewed.** `packages/domain` (tax engine, numbering, turnover, GSTR-1, GSTR-3B, pre-filing checks, GSTR-2B reconciliation, e-invoice payload, e-way bill), `packages/gsp`, the data files under `packages/domain/data`, the cancellation and filing flows in `packages/db`, the print field list, and the seven existing review sheets. The full unit suites were run before and after the changes below.

## 1. Findings and what was changed

| #   | Area                      | Finding                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   | Severity | Action                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| --- | ------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| F-1 | TCS s.206C(1H)            | The engine stopped TCS on sale of goods from **1 October 2024**, citing the Finance (No. 2) Act 2024. That Act did not remove 206C(1H). It was the **Finance Act 2025** that omitted s.206C(1H) with effect from **1 April 2025**. For receipts between 1 October 2024 and 31 March 2025 the software would have under-collected TCS for a seller above the INR 50 lakh threshold.                        | High     | **Fixed.** `TCS_206C1H_SUNSET` is `2025-04-01` in `packages/domain/src/tax/document.ts`; vector "TCS omitted from 1 Apr 2025" and a new boundary vector "TCS still collected on 31 Mar 2025"; receipt-side tests, the generator, the README, and the English and Hindi screen strings updated. |
| F-2 | Deemed exports            | A deemed export (s.147; Notification 48/2017-CT: supply to an EOU, against advance authorisation or EPCG) was always charged **IGST**. A deemed export is a taxable supply, not a zero-rated one: the heads follow the ordinary intra- or inter-state test (s.7 and s.8 IGST Act) and the tax is refunded afterwards under s.54. A supply to an EOU in the supplier's own state must carry CGST and SGST. | High     | **Fixed.** `taxHeadsFor` takes the place of supply and applies the ordinary test for a deemed export; the document builder passes it through. Tests added. GSTR-1 treatment (B2B with the deemed-export invoice type) was already right.                                                       |
| F-3 | Credit notes              | Nothing warned when a credit note was dated after the **30 November following the end of the financial year of the supply**, the last date on which it can reduce output tax (s.34(2), as amended from 1 October 2022).                                                                                                                                                                                   | Medium   | **Fixed.** New pre-filing check `credit_note_after_deadline` (table 9B) using the linked invoice date; helper `creditNoteDeadline`. It is a warning that must be acknowledged before export. Debit notes carry no such limit for the supplier and are not checked.                             |
| F-4 | Cancellation after filing | A document in a period whose GSTR-1 had been marked filed could still be **cancelled** by an owner with a lock override. Once a number has been reported in GSTR-1 the only lawful correction is a credit note (s.34) or an amendment; "cancelling" it in the books would make the books disagree with the return.                                                                                        | Medium   | **Fixed.** `cancelDocument` now refuses when any filed return (GSTR-1 first) covers the document's date, with a message that points at a credit note. No override. Repository test added. A manual period lock without a filing still allows an owner override, as before.                     |
| F-5 | e-Way bill, document age  | Since **1 January 2025** the portal does not generate an e-way bill for a document older than **180 days**, and extensions are capped at **360 days** from generation (GSTN advisory, 17 December 2024). The rules data and validator did not know this; a user would only learn it from the portal error.                                                                                                | Low      | **Fixed.** `docAge` added to the e-way bill rules; `checkEwb` warns when told today's date. Test added. State-wise intra-state limits for Bihar, Punjab, Jharkhand and Rajasthan added as **unchecked** entries (INR 1 lakh), so they do not change behaviour until confirmed (B-112).         |

The remaining findings are notes and backlog items, listed in sections 3 and 4.

## 2. Points in the earlier sheets, with this reviewer's decision

### Tax engine (`tax-engine-review.md`)

| Open point                            | Decision                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| ------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1 B2CL threshold                      | **Confirmed.** INR 1 lakh for invoices from 1 August 2024 (Notification 12/2024-CT); INR 2.5 lakh before. The test is on invoice value including tax.                                                                                                                                                                 |
| 2 206C(1H) sunset                     | **Corrected** (F-1). Advances received before 1 April 2025 for supplies made later: TCS was collectible on receipt under the old provision, so the receipt date governs. The software uses the receipt date, which is right.                                                                                          |
| 3 Tax-inclusive drift                 | **Accepted** with a condition: the printed invoice must show the heads as computed (they may differ from the entered price by up to two paise in total). Section 170 rounding applies to the amounts payable in the return, not to each invoice line. No change.                                                      |
| 4 Fractional head rates               | **Needs a change before GA.** 0.25% (rough diamonds, 7102) splits to 0.125% per head, which an integer basis-point column cannot hold; 0.1% merchant-export (5 bp per head) is fine. Store rates in hundredths of a basis point, or hold the total rate and derive the heads at print and return time. Backlog B-119. |
| 5 Deemed exports                      | **Corrected** (F-2).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| 6 Composition purchases, RCM on sales | **Accepted for now**, with one addition: a composition supplier cannot make an inter-state outward supply (s.10(2)(c)); the editor should block it. Backlog B-120.                                                                                                                                                    |

### Numbering and cancellation (`numbering-and-cancellation.md`)

Reversal entries on cancellation are dated on the day of cancellation. **Accepted**, because F-4 now prevents cancellation after the period is filed, so a cancellation always lands in an unfiled period and the original and reversal fall before the same return. If the owner later unfiles a period (`reopen`), the lock remains and the ordinary override rules apply.

### Invoice fields (`invoice-fields.md`)

1. Recipient address when none is recorded: **accepted below INR 50,000** for unregistered recipients (Rule 46(e) asks for the address only at or above that value); for registered recipients Rule 46(d) requires the address, so the editor should insist on one when the party has a GSTIN. Backlog B-121.
2. Place of supply printed on intra-state invoices: **accepted**; Rule 46(m) requires it for inter-state supplies and does not forbid it otherwise.
3. Amount in words in English for languages other than English and Hindi: **accepted** for the beta.
4. Export invoices must carry the endorsement wording of the third proviso to Rule 46 ("SUPPLY MEANT FOR EXPORT ON PAYMENT OF INTEGRATED TAX" or "SUPPLY MEANT FOR EXPORT UNDER BOND OR LETTER OF UNDERTAKING WITHOUT PAYMENT OF INTEGRATED TAX") and the **name of the country of destination**; the same endorsement with "SUPPLY TO SEZ UNIT OR SEZ DEVELOPER FOR AUTHORISED OPERATIONS" for SEZ supplies. The templates print a short label ("Export under LUT"). **Must change before GA**: print the full endorsement and the destination country. Backlog B-122.

### GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B (`gstr-review.md`)

All seven earlier decisions **stand**. Additional context that the Filing Desk help text should carry (B-123): from the July 2025 tax period GSTR-3B's auto-populated outward liability is **locked** and can only be changed through **GSTR-1A** before filing GSTR-3B, and table 3.2 is non-editable; returns cannot be filed more than **three years** after their due date (in force from 1 July 2025); amendments of the current period belong in GSTR-1A rather than waiting for the next GSTR-1.

### GSTR-2B (`gstr2b-review.md`)

All seven earlier decisions **stand**. Two additions: (a) since October 2024 the portal's **Invoice Management System (IMS)** drives 2B: a recipient accepts, rejects or keeps pending each supplier document, and 2B is drafted from those actions. The reconciliation classes map naturally onto IMS actions (matched → accept, mismatched → pending, not in books → reject) and the product should let the user export or push those actions once a GSP exposes IMS (B-124). (b) **Rule 37** (reversal of credit when the supplier is not paid within 180 days, with re-availment on payment) is not modelled; with party ledgers available it can be computed. Backlog B-125.

### e-Invoice and e-way bill (`einvoice-review.md`)

Decisions stand. The INR 5 crore threshold (Notification 10/2023-CT, 1 August 2023), the 24-hour IRN cancellation window and the 30-day reporting limit for AATO of INR 10 crore and above from 1 April 2025 are **confirmed**. Row 14 (state limits) now includes four more unchecked states, and the 180-day document-age rule is in the data (F-5). Nothing here replaces the sandbox run (B-111).

## 3. Confirmed as correct (no change)

- Intra-state split CGST + SGST; CGST + UTGST in Andaman and Nicobar, Chandigarh, Dadra and Nagar Haveli and Daman and Diu, Ladakh, Lakshadweep; SGST in Delhi, Puducherry, Jammu and Kashmir.
- Zero-rating of exports and SEZ supplies with and without payment of tax (s.16 IGST Act); LUT handling.
- Reverse charge computed and reported but not charged; RCM self-invoice for purchases from unregistered suppliers.
- Invoice-level discount allocated pro rata (s.15(3)); cess ad valorem and specific; composition supplier collects no tax and prints the Rule 46A declaration.
- TDS s.194Q on taxable value above INR 50 lakh and its exclusivity with TCS (CBDT Circular 13/2021).
- Document number rule (Rule 46(b)): at most 16 characters, letters, digits, `-` and `/`, unique within the financial year; number assigned at issue; cancel-never-delete with the number retained and reported in table 13.
- GSTR-1 table mapping (B2B, B2CL with the INR 1 lakh test, B2CS, CDNR, CDNUR, EXP, NIL, HSN table 12 with the B2B and B2C split, DOCS table 13); credit notes not required to link to a single original invoice.
- GSTR-3B 3.1(b) carrying exports and SEZ supplies; table 4 reporting ineligible credit in 4A and reversing it in 4B(1); 4D for credit not available in 2B.
- Due dates: GSTR-1 on the 11th, GSTR-3B on the 20th; QRMP GSTR-1 on the 13th after the quarter and GSTR-3B on the 22nd (category X states) or 24th; the category X list in the data is correct.
- HSN digits: 4 for aggregate turnover up to INR 5 crore, 6 above (Notification 78/2020-CT), computed from the previous year's books.
- e-way bill: INR 50,000 consignment value; one day per 200 km (20 km over-dimensional); Part B not needed for up to 50 km to the transporter within the state; cancellation within 24 hours; extension 8 hours either side of expiry.
- HSN master `2025.9.1` reflects the rate structure from **22 September 2025** (0, 5, 18 and 40 percent). It is a 112-code starter and unreviewed (B-001); that is a GA blocker, not a correctness defect in what is there.
