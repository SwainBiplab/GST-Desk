# e-Invoice and e-way bill: reviewer sign-off

**Status: NOT SIGNED.** Nothing here has been run against the live e-invoice or e-way bill portal, and the field list, error codes and limits come from public documentation, not from a sandbox. The data files carry `verified: false`. Do not turn e-invoicing on for a real business in a real GSP mode until this sheet is signed and `einvoice-sandbox-run.md` is complete. Practice mode (the default) sends nothing.

Decisions below were taken by Claude in the reviewer's role, as the user asked ("decide it, think yourself as a real CA"). They are recorded with their legal basis so a licensed chartered accountant can accept or overturn each one. Claude is not a licensed CA and signs nothing. **The section references are from memory of the CGST Rules and the portals' published guidance and must be confirmed against the current text by the signing accountant.**

Scope: `packages/domain/data/einvoice/rules-v1_1.ts`, `data/ewb-rules.ts`, `data/gsp-errors.ts`, `packages/domain/src/einvoice/`, `packages/db/src/repositories/einvoice.ts`, `packages/gsp/`, `apps/web/src/features/einvoice/`.

## Decisions taken

| #   | Rule as built                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 | Basis (to confirm)                                                       | Test                                                            | Reviewed |
| --- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------- | -------- |
| 1   | e-Invoicing is **off** until the owner turns it on; the Settings tab shows last year's turnover and says it is required from INR 5 crore. The software never decides this for the owner.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      | Rule 48(4) CGST Rules and the turnover notification as amended (5 crore) | web `settings` e2e                                              |          |
| 2   | The turnover test in the law looks at **any preceding year since 2017-18**; the hint here looks at last year only. A business that crossed the limit earlier and fell below is still bound. The owner decides.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                | same                                                                     | n/a (hint only)                                                 |          |
| 3   | Documents needing an IRN: tax invoice, credit note and debit note to a **registered** buyer (B2B, SEZ with or without payment, exports with or without payment). Deemed exports are not offered yet (B-113). Not for bills of supply to consumers, delivery challans, or documents of unregistered buyers.                                                                                                                                    | Rule 48; exemptions for B2C                                              | domain `needsIrn`                                               |          |
| 4   | Document number: up to 16 characters, letters, digits, `/` and `-` only, not starting with `0`, `/` or `-`; unique per GSTIN, document type and financial year.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               | NIC e-invoice schema 1.1                                                 | domain "document number"                                        |          |
| 5   | Reporting age: for a business with turnover of INR 10 crore and above, an invoice older than 30 days cannot be reported (from 2025-04-01). The check applies it from the date in the data file.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               | GSTN advisory of January 2025                                            | domain "too old"                                                |          |
| 6   | HSN digits: 4 up to INR 5 crore last year, 6 above; the check accepts 4, 6 or 8 digits and asks for at least 6 above INR 5 crore.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             | Notification 78/2020-CT                                                  | domain HSN tests                                                |          |
| 7   | Each line's tax is recomputed in integer paise from the assessable value and the rate and must equal the stored tax within INR 1; the total must equal the sum of lines plus charges plus round-off within INR 1 (round-off at most INR 99.99).                                                                                                                                                                                               | Schema validation of the portal (values must reconcile)                  | domain "line total", "round-off"                                |          |
| 8   | Inter-state: only IGST; intra-state: only CGST and SGST. Export and SEZ are inter-state with place of supply 96 (export) or the SEZ state.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    | Sections 7, 8 and 12 IGST Act; schema                                    | domain "heads"                                                  |          |
| 9   | A supply without payment of tax (`SEZWOP`, `EXPWOP`) cannot carry tax. **The software does not check that a LUT exists**; the owner is responsible for having one. The shipping bill number, date and port code are not collected yet (B-113).                                                                                                                                                                                                | Rule 96A                                                                 | domain "without payment"                                        |          |
| 10  | A credit or debit note must name the invoice it corrects (number and date).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   | Section 34 CGST Act; schema `PrecDocDtls`                                | domain "preceding"                                              |          |
| 11  | An IRN can be cancelled **within 24 hours** of its acknowledgement; the document is cancelled with it; after that the screen points to a credit note. A cancelled IRN's number can never be reused (the portal answers 2150 again; the practice portal does too).                                                                                                                                                                             | Rule 48 (cancellation window)                                            | db "cancels an IRN within 24 hours", gsp                        |          |
| 12  | An IRN cannot be cancelled while its e-way bill is active.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    | Portal rule (error 2230)                                                 | db "refuses to cancel an IRN while…"                            |          |
| 13  | After an IRN the document cannot be revised financially or cancelled directly; only the two routes in 11 are open. The database refuses, not only the screen.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 | Rule 48 (the IRN is the invoice's identity)                              | db "generates an IRN through the queue"                         |          |
| 14  | E-way bill is needed when the consignment value is **above INR 50,000** (all of the invoice's value, tax included). Some states have a higher intra-state limit. INR 1 lakh is listed for Delhi, Maharashtra, Tamil Nadu and West Bengal but is **unverified** (`checked: false`), and a state's limit is applied only once it is checked: until then the national limit applies everywhere, which errs on the side of making a bill (B-112). | Rule 138(1), Rule 138(14) and state notifications                        | domain "threshold" (including a checked and an unchecked state) |          |
| 15  | Validity: one day per 200 km (one per 20 km for over-dimensional cargo), each day ending at midnight of the following day, so a bill made at 18:00 on the 3rd for 100 km runs to 23:59:59 on the 4th.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         | Rule 138(10) and its Explanation                                         | domain "validity", db "makes, extends, re-routes…"              |          |
| 16  | An e-way bill is extended from **8 hours before to 8 hours after** it expires; the person gives the distance still to go and where the goods are.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             | Rule 138(10) proviso and the CBIC notification on extension              | domain `ewbExtendable`, web e2e                                 |          |
| 17  | An e-way bill is cancelled within 24 hours of being made, and only if the goods did not move or moved otherwise than stated.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  | Rule 138(9)                                                              | domain `ewbCancellable`                                         |          |
| 18  | Part B (vehicle) is not asked for when the distance is known, within one state, and up to 50 km; otherwise a vehicle number (or a transport document for rail, air or ship) is required.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      | Rule 138(5), Rule 138(3) and the 50 km exemption                         | domain "Part B"                                                 |          |
| 19  | Vehicle numbers are accepted in the common formats (`MH12AB1234`, the BH series, and temporary numbers beginning `TR`); anything else is refused before sending.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              | Portal validation                                                        | domain `normaliseVehicle`                                       |          |
| 20  | Every request to the portal and every answer is kept in a log with passwords, secrets and tokens removed; an IRN, QR and e-way bill are kept with the document.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               | Records to be kept (Section 36 CGST Act)                                 | db "logs the call without secrets"                              |          |
