# Numbering, cancellation and audit: reviewer sign-off

Scope: how document numbers are given, how an issued document is cancelled or corrected, the period lock, and the audit trail. Code is in `packages/domain/src/numbering.ts`, `packages/domain/src/series.ts`, `packages/db/src/repositories/{numbering,period-lock,document-flow}.ts` and `apps/web/src/features/{numbering,documents}`. Each row is a rule, what the software does, and the test that proves it. The last column is for the reviewer.

## Rule 46(b), CGST Rules: serial number of a tax invoice

Rule 46(b) requires "a consecutive serial number, not exceeding sixteen characters, in one or multiple series, containing alphabets or numerals or special characters hyphen or dash and slash symbolised as "-" and "/" respectively, and any combination thereof, unique for a financial year".

## Cancellation

The approach: **a cancelled document keeps its number and stays on record; it is excluded from turnover and returns; the ledger and stock it posted are reversed with contra entries (nothing is deleted); payments applied to it are unapplied and the user is warned.** Once a document has been shared with the customer, the better path is a credit note (section 34), and the screen says so before cancelling.

## Several devices (groundwork, not yet live)

When a business is in synced mode, a device may only use numbers from a range reserved for it (`number_reservations`). With no range available, it numbers documents `PROV-0001`, `PROV-0002`, … which are replaced with real numbers when it reconnects (`finaliseNumber`, used by sync in prompt 41). Ranges are carved out of the shared counter so two devices never get the same number; unused numbers are returned to the pool. See ADR 0006. A **provisional number is not a valid tax invoice number** and must not be shared with a customer; the sync work must finalise before a document leaves the device.

## Open points for the reviewer

1. **Date of the reversing entries on cancellation.** The software dates the contra entries on the day of cancellation. A cancellation in a later month than the original document leaves the original in its own month's ledger and the reversal in the later month. Turnover and GST reports use document status, so the cancelled document is excluded from its original period. Confirm this is acceptable for the books, or whether reversals should be dated on the original date.
2. **Gaps.** A cancelled number is never reused, so it stays in the sequence as a cancelled document. Numbers handed back from an unused device range may leave gaps. Confirm that gaps explained by cancelled documents and unused ranges are acceptable under Rule 46(b) ("consecutive").
3. **Credit note versus cancellation after sharing.** The screen explains the difference but does not block cancelling a document that has been shared. Confirm that advice-only is enough.
4. **Editing a document after issue.** The software allows financial edits (with a revision counter and full audit) when the period is open and no e-invoice exists. Confirm this is acceptable, or whether financial edits after issue should always be a credit note and a new invoice.
