# Tax invoice fields: reviewer sign-off (Rule 46, CGST Rules)

Scope: what the **Classic** template prints on a tax invoice, for intra-state, inter-state, export and reverse-charge supplies. Code: `packages/print` (`model.ts`, `stored.ts`, `layout.ts`). The test that prints real documents and checks every row below is `packages/db/src/print-rule46.test.ts`; it uses seeded documents for intra-state and inter-state supplies, and documents made through the repositories for export and reverse charge (the demo data has neither). The last column is for the reviewer.

An issued document is printed from what was **stored when it was issued**, not recalculated, so the printed amounts are exactly the amounts on record.

## Open points for the reviewer

1. **Recipient address when none is recorded.** A party saved without an address prints its name, GSTIN and state only. Confirm that is acceptable for invoices below the threshold for which Rule 46(e) asks for an address.
2. **Place of supply on intra-state invoices.** It is printed on every invoice (not only inter-state ones). Confirm that is fine.
3. **Amount in words** is Indian-grouped English or Hindi; in other languages the English words print. Confirm acceptable until the other languages are added.
4. **Label translations** in Hindi, Tamil, Bengali, Gujarati and Marathi are drafts (`docs/qa/print-language-review.md`).
