Do not rely on the reconciliation for a real return until this sheet is signed. The screen says so.

Scope: importing GSTR-2B, matching it with purchase bills, deciding each result, applying the outcome to GSTR-3B table 4, and the pre-filing checks that block export. ## What must be checked first: the file layouts

The portal does not publish a downloadable schema for the 2B file. The JSON parser (`parse.ts`, layout "v1") reads the structure documented for the portal download: `data.docdata.b2b[].inv[]` and `cdnr[].nt[]`, with `inum`, `dt`, `val`, `itcavl`, `rsn`, and amounts either at invoice level or in `items[]`. The Excel reader finds the table by its column headings (GSTIN, invoice number, date, taxable value, the tax heads, ITC availability). Both were written from that documentation and from the headings suppliers and the portal use; **neither has been run on a real downloaded file.** Sections other than invoices and credit and debit notes (amendments, import of goods, input service distributors, e-commerce) are not imported, and the import says so.

## Pre-filing checks (all block export until fixed or acknowledged with a reason)

| Code                                | What it finds                                                                                                                             |
| ----------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `duplicate_invoice`                 | The same buyer, date and amount on two documents with different numbers.                                                                  |
| `b2c_above_threshold_no_gstin`      | A sale to a buyer with no GSTIN at or above the limit the business set for asking (off when the limit is 0).                              |
| `supply_type_pos_mismatch`          | Marked intra-state but the place of supply is another state, or the reverse.                                                              |
| `pos_differs_from_party_state`      | The place of supply is not the buyer's state (a warning: it can be right when goods ship elsewhere).                                      |
| `hsn_invalid_length`, `missing_hsn` | An HSN that is not 2, 4, 6 or 8 digits, or none.                                                                                          |
| `rate_not_matching_hsn`             | A rate the HSN list does not allow for that code. **One problem per HSN and rate**, listing the documents; one acknowledgement covers it. |
| `negative_value`                    | A negative taxable value.                                                                                                                 |
| `credit_note_exceeds_invoice`       | A credit note larger than the invoice it refers to.                                                                                       |
| `cancelled_with_payments`           | A cancelled document with a payment still recorded against it.                                                                            |
| `series_gap`, `date_out_of_order`   | A missing number in a series, or a number dated earlier than the one before it.                                                           |
| `date_in_future`                    | A document dated after today.                                                                                                             |

An acknowledgement is kept with its reason in the audit trail and can be taken back. The export is refused again by the database layer, not only by the screen, so an out-of-date screen cannot get past it.

## Open points for the reviewer

1. **Tolerances**: are 3 days, 45 days and INR 1 or 0.5% sensible defaults?
2. **Not eligible**: a 2B line the portal marks "not available" reduces credit and is shown in 4D(2). Confirm, and whether the reason code should be kept.
3. **Deferred credit**: a bill not yet in 2B is taken out of credit for the month and claimed when it appears (carried forward). Confirm that this matches the practice of claiming credit only as shown in 2B.
4. **Reverse-charge bills** are left out of the matching because their credit does not come from 2B. Confirm.
5. **Rate check against the HSN list**: the starter list is unreviewed (B-001), so a warning may be wrong because the list is. Confirm that acknowledging with a reason is the right way to handle that.
6. **Excess claimed**: tax in the books above what the supplier reported is taken out as "claims more tax than the supplier reported". Confirm.
