# 0012 — The review inbox and the Tier 0 assistants

Status: accepted. Context: prompt 21, PRD FR-171, FR-175, FR-177, FR-178, UX-02, Principle 3 (AI proposes, people dispose).

## Decision

- **One inbox, one contract.** Everything the app wants a person to look at is a `review_items` row: a `kind`, the data it is about (`payload_json`), the proposal (`suggestion_json`), a diff, a confidence and who raised it. Twelve kinds are named (`INBOX_KINDS`); eight are raised by rules today (duplicate party, duplicate item, HSN suggestion, GST rate change, payment reminder, low stock, old draft, failed backup). `ai_extraction` (prompt 36), `prefiling_issue` and `reconciliation_mismatch` (phase 2) and `sync_conflict` (phase 3) show what they hold, but Accept stays off with a note saying which prompt builds it. Nothing is faked.
- **Rules are plain functions of data.** Each detector (`packages/ai/src/inbox/detectors.ts`) takes rows that have already been loaded and returns findings. No model, no network, no clock except the `now` it is given. They are browser-safe and tested with fixtures. The part that touches the database (`packages/ai/src/db/inbox.ts`, `runTier0`) loads what each rule needs through the existing repositories and nothing else.
- **A finding is named by a key, and the key makes a run safe to repeat.** `duplicate_party:<idA>:<idB>` (ids sorted), `hsn_suggestion:<item>:<code>`, `rate_change:<item>:<rate>:<cess>:<from>`, `draft_stale:<doc>`, `reminder_due:<party>:<ISO week>`, `low_stock:<item>:<month>`, `backup_failed:<time>`. `reconcile` compares the findings of a run with every key ever raised: a key seen in **any** state is never raised again (a person who said no is not asked again), a new key is created, and a waiting item whose key has gone is closed as `expired` ("no longer needed"). Reminders and low stock carry the week or month so they come back at most that often. A kind whose rule did not run is never treated as "all clear".
- **Lifecycle.** `pending → accepted | rejected | expired`; all three ends are final. Snooze is not a status: it is `snoozed_until` on a pending item, so the item returns by itself when the time passes and nothing has to wake it up. The bell, the nav badge and the dashboard count open items only (`snoozed_until` empty or past).
- **Accepting applies through the feature that owns the data, in one transaction.** `applyReviewItem` calls `PartyRepo.merge`, `ItemRepo.merge` (new), `ItemRepo.update` (HSN, GST rate and cess) or `ReminderRepo.create`, and marks the item accepted in the same transaction (`acceptReviewItem`), so a failure leaves both the data and the item as they were. A bulk accept is the same call over many items inside one outer transaction: all or none. A change of rate on an item affects documents made afterwards only; issued documents keep their stored amounts, so there is nothing to recompute (the engine computes a document's money when it is made, and it is not asked to here). Informational kinds (low stock, old draft, failed backup) only acknowledge.
- **Nothing is applied on its own.** The one automatic rule that exists — accept HSN suggestions at or above a chosen confidence — is a setting (`inbox.autoRules`) that is off until a person picks a percentage. AI actors cannot accept at all (`assertHuman`). Accepting a reminder records that it was handled; sending it (WhatsApp link) is a separate button the person presses.
- **Edit, then accept.** A person can change the HSN code, the reminder text, or which of two duplicates is kept. The edited value is validated against the same schema, applied instead of the suggestion, and the difference is stored in `correction_json`.
- **Evals use what people accepted.** `pnpm ai:eval --schema hsnSuggestion --accepted <file.sqlite>` adds the HSN suggestions accepted in a local database (with the person's correction applied) as extra cases, read-only. Nothing leaves the machine.
- **Item merge is new and has no undo yet.** `ItemRepo.merge` moves document lines, stock movements, batches, variants and files to the kept item, fills what the kept item lacks (HSN, SKU, barcode, rates, reorder level), archives the other and audits every moved row. Stock follows because it is the sum of movements. A product and a service are never merged. An undo like the party one is in the backlog (B-068); until then the archived item is in the trash for 30 days.
- **The job runner is small on purpose.** `InboxJobs` runs all rules shortly after the app opens and every six hours while it stays open (checked every 30 minutes against a last-run time kept per business), and runs the duplicate and HSN rules about three seconds after a party or item is saved. One run at a time per business shares its result. A run on the demo book (264 findings) takes about 170 ms, and a repeat run about 25 ms. HSN lookups skip items whose suggestion was already answered.
- **Reminder wording** is in six languages (`packages/i18n/src/reminder.ts`) and is chosen from the customer's state, because parties have no language field yet (B-069). The non-English texts are machine drafts and are flagged for review (B-070); a person reads and can edit every message before it is sent.
- **Scale.** The list is virtualised, and the e2e suite loads 5,000 waiting items (`?demo=1&inbox=5000`) and checks that only a window of rows is drawn and that jumping to the end takes under two seconds.

## Consequences

- Adding a kind means one schema, one detector, one apply case and one line in `KIND_INFO`; a test checks every kind has info and a schema.
- Detectors are blocked into small groups before comparing (same GSTIN, same phone, same first letters of the name), so 5,000 parties do not mean 12.5 million comparisons. A group of more than 200 is skipped, which could hide a duplicate among very many near-identical names; it is the cheap, safe side of the trade.
- A finding that clears and later returns has the same key and stays closed. Keys that carry a value (the rate, the week, the month) are the escape hatch, and are chosen per kind for that reason.
- Keyboard: J/K move, A accept, E edit, R reject (asks for an optional reason), S snooze, O open the record. They work anywhere on the page unless a field has focus. After an accept the next item moves up, so clearing a queue is repeated presses of A: 20 accepts took well under the two-minute target in the e2e run.
