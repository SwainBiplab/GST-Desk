# 0010 — Basic inventory

Status: accepted. Context: prompt 19, PRD FR-110, UX-06.

## Decision

- **Stock is the sum of movements.** Nothing stores "current stock". An item's quantity is the sum of its `stock_movements`, so it cannot disagree with them. Opening stock is a movement too (written when the item is created), which means a report or a screen never has to add an "opening" column on top. This also fixes a double count in the first version of the stock summary, which added the item's opening quantity to movements that already held it.
- **Documents write movements in the issue transaction.** A tax invoice, bill of supply, debit note and purchase return take stock out; a purchase bill and a credit note bring it in; quotations, proformas, receipts and expenses move nothing. The rule is one function, `stockEffectOf` in the domain package, and the seed uses it too, so a test can rebuild every item's stock from the documents alone and compare it with the movements (`inventory.test.ts`, on SQLite and Postgres). Cancelling reverses the net of a document's movements with opposite movements and keeps both on record. Editing an issued document reverses and rewrites them (as before).
- **Delivery challans move stock only when set to.** A challan has two options kept with the document: "take the goods out of stock" and "goods will come back" (returnable). An invoice made from a challan that already took the goods out does not take them out a second time.
- **Inventory can be switched off.** The caller passes `inventoryEnabled` from the business's feature flags, as before. While it is off no movements are written and no stock screens, alerts or report show; the movements already written are kept, so switching it on again shows the same stock. A sale made while it was off moved nothing, and stays that way.
- **Negative stock is a per-business policy.** `allow` (the default) warns and lets the person decide: the editor asks before issuing a document that would take an item below zero. `block` refuses it, and the refusal is made inside the issue transaction (`InsufficientStockError`), so a document that is refused stays a draft with no number, no ledger entry and no movement. All lines of the same item count together, items that do not track stock and documents that bring stock in are ignored. **Cancelling is never refused**: it corrects a mistake, and cancelling a purchase whose goods were already sold simply leaves the item negative, which the screens show.
- **Adjustments are movements with a reason.** `InventoryRepo.adjust` books stock in or out for many items in one transaction (reasons: count, damage, loss, found, correction, other), each audited, refused inside a locked period, and all-or-nothing. A physical count (`applyCount`) works out each difference from the movements inside the transaction, so a sale made a moment ago is not overwritten, and leaves items that already match alone. Opening stock can be corrected afterwards the same way (backlog B-007).
- **Count sheets are plain CSV.** The sheet to fill in lists every item that tracks stock with what the books say and a blank "Counted quantity". Coming back, items are found by SKU, barcode or name, blank counts mean "not counted", and every row that cannot be used is reported with its line number (no item, not a number, negative, listed twice, no match, more than one match, item does not track stock). The file is read and previewed before anything is booked.
- **Alerts.** The reorder level is the item's existing minimum stock. Items below it feed the dashboard's low-stock card, the notification bell (a small menu: review items, and low stock) and a "below reorder level" filter on the stock summary report. The seed gives its tracked items reorder levels so the demo shows some.
- **Stock summary** now reports quantity and value at the purchase price (closing quantity times the purchase rate), as the prompt asked (backlog B-044 is narrowed to godowns, batches and other valuation methods).

## Consequences

- The demo business has inventory switched on, so its documents write movements and its items show their stock.
- A document that would take stock negative asks first under `allow`; in the editor this is one extra dialog only in that case.
- Stock is valued at the item's current purchase price, not at what each unit cost. Cost-based valuation (FIFO, weighted average), batches, serials and godowns are out of scope until prompts 51 and 52.
- The bell used to be a link to the inbox; it is now a menu with the inbox as its first entry.
