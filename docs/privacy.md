# Privacy

GST Desk keeps your business on your own computer. This page says exactly what the app does with information, in plain words. The same facts are in the app under Settings, Privacy.

## Your business data

Your parties, items, documents, payments and settings are stored in a database on your own device: in the browser's storage for the web app, in a folder of yours (`%APPDATA%\GST Desk` on Windows, `~/Library/Application Support/GST Desk` on macOS, `~/.local/share/gst-desk` on Linux) for the desktop app. It is not sent to us or to anyone else. It leaves your device only when **you** make a copy: a backup you download or save to a folder, an export, a document you share, or a copy to your own Google Drive or OneDrive that you set up yourself (desktop only).

## Usage counters (off by default)

If you turn on "Share anonymous usage counts", the app counts how many times each of a short fixed list of features was used, for example `invoice_issued` or `report_opened`, and nothing else.

- It never counts or sends anything you type: no names, GSTIN, phone numbers, amounts, document numbers, file names or notes.
- The only things that can be counted are the sixteen feature names listed in Settings, Privacy. Anything not on that list is thrown away before it is stored.
- What would be sent is shown, exactly, on that screen: a version number, "web" or "desktop", a random id for this install (not made from anything about you or your computer), and the counts.
- It is sent about once a week, with no cookies and no referrer. If the copy of the app you have was built without a collection address, nothing is sent at all and the counts only stay on your device (the screen says so).
- Turning it off deletes the counts waiting to be sent and the random id. "Start again with a new random id" does the same for the id.
- It is off until you turn it on. Nothing is counted, stored or sent while it is off.

## Reporting a problem (desktop)

"Report a problem" (Settings, Desktop) saves a small ZIP of the app's recent log files and a line about your system (the app version, operating system and processor type). Before the logs go in, email addresses, GSTINs, PANs, ids, long numbers and your computer's user name are taken out. It never contains your database, backups, documents or settings. You choose where to save it and whether to send it.

## Updates (desktop)

The desktop app asks the release server whether a newer version exists, about once a day. The request names the app and its version, as any update check must. Nothing about your business is in it. An update is installed only after you choose Restart to update, and only if it is signed with our key.

## Your rights over your data

- **Take everything out:** Settings, Backup, Export as JSON or CSV files, or make a backup. These are yours to keep.
- **Delete everything:** in the browser, Settings, Privacy, "Delete all my data on this device". On the desktop, close the app and delete the data folder shown under Settings, Desktop.
- **Language and translation:** the app does not send your text anywhere to translate it. Transliteration (typing a name in Hindi letters) runs on your device.
