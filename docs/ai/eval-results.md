## Local models (`llama`, `ollama`)

Not run on this machine: there is no model and no GPU here, and the desktop glue that starts the
sidecar is unverified (B-150). To record a model's numbers, start `llama-server` (or Ollama) with the
model and run:

```
pnpm ai:eval --provider llama --url http://127.0.0.1:8080 --model qwen2.5-1.5b-instruct-q4 --markdown
pnpm ai:eval --provider ollama --model qwen2.5:1.5b --markdown
```

and paste the tables here with the machine (CPU, memory) and the model version. The acceptance
criterion in the prompt (at least 80% on clean printed bills with the recommended model on the baseline
device, and a CA spot-check of 20 accepted bills) is open until that is done: see B-151 and B-152.

## What the Tier 0 misses are

- `lines[].gstRateBp`: the plain and lowercase-retail layouts print no rate per line and a tax summary
  with two or more rates, so the rate cannot be known from the bill alone. The extractor leaves it at 0
  (unknown) and lowers the lines' confidence; the HSN master fills it where the HSN is printed (the
  fixture master covers 49 codes). In the app the catalogue item's rate takes over once a line is matched.
- `supplierName` (3 of 63): the lowercase-retail layout puts the city on the same line as the name.
- `supplierGstin` (2 of 63): a GSTIN written in lowercase on a bill whose text also holds the buyer's.
- `totalPaise` (3 of 63): a bill whose last "total" line is the amount in words.
