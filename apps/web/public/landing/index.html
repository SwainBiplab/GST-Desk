Make the bill. GST is already done.

GST Desk is billing and books for small Indian businesses. It works out the tax, keeps every number in order, and keeps your data on your own computer, with or without internet.

Open the web app See what works today

The web app runs in this browser and keeps your books in this browser on this computer. A Windows desktop installer (about 8 MB, installs for one user, no administrator rights) is not released yet. macOS and Linux builds are planned.

What it helps you do

  • Bill without doing the tax maths

    Pick a customer and items. GST, CGST/SGST or IGST, rounding and amount in words are worked out for you, and printed on an invoice you can send.

  • Know who owes you

    Record payments and receipts against bills. See what each customer still owes and which bills are overdue.

  • Keep honest records

    Bill numbers run in order. A bill is cancelled, never deleted, and every change is kept in an audit trail.

  • See how the business is doing

    A dashboard and reports for sales, tax, stock and outstanding, ready to export for your accountant.

Where GST Desk is different

  • Hindi fully translated, and you can type names in Indian scripts by typing English letters.
  • Invoice and print labels in six languages.
  • A review inbox that only suggests (duplicates, missing HSN codes, overdue customers). Nothing changes until you accept.
  • Open exports (JSON, CSV) and verified backups, with a trash you can restore from.
  • Usage counters are off by default; if you opt in, they hold counts only, never your content.
  • Small per-user Windows installer, about 8 MB, no administrator rights.

Where they are ahead today

  • Filing support: e-invoice, e-way bill and GST return workflows.
  • They are established products used by many businesses; we are a new beta.
  • Several users and phone apps.
  • Wider accounting features, for example TallyPrime also lists payroll and banking. We plan fuller accounting for a later phase.

Vendor details were read from their own pages in November 2026: Tally prices, Tally FAQ, Vyapar, Zoho Books and myBillBook. Plans and prices change, so confirm there before you decide. Trade-off, stated plainly: a file on your computer needs your own backups (the app makes them easy) and has no multi-device sync yet.
